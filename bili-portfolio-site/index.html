About

More than a videographer. Not pretending to be everything.

My foundation is video production and editing. Over time, my role expanded into managing the broader system around content: website structure, SEO implementation, campaign execution, publishing, and AI-supported workflows.

I work best where creative output needs to connect with a real commercial objective. That means understanding the audience, organizing the process, collaborating with sales and marketing, and making sure the final asset has a clear next step.
